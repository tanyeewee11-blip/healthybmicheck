Common Questions

Is the BMI Calculator accurate?

While BMI is a useful screening tool to see if you are in a healthy weight range, it doesn't directly measure body fat. It's a general indicator and may not be accurate for athletes or individuals with high muscle mass.

How many calories should I eat a day?

Daily calorie needs vary based on age, sex, weight, and activity level. Our Calorie Calculator uses the Mifflin-St Jeor Equation to provide a highly accurate estimate for your daily maintenance calories.

Is this health tool free?

Yes, all tools on HealthyBMICheck.com are 100% free to use. We do not store your personal health data; all calculations are performed locally in your web browser for maximum privacy.
