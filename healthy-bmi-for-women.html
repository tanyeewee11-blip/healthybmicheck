What is a Healthy BMI for Women?

Healthy BMI range is 18.5–24.9 for most women.

Note: Asian women may have higher risk at BMI 23+

Why Women Have Higher Body Fat

Women naturally store more essential fat for hormonal function.

Hormones & Menopause

Hormonal changes shift fat distribution to the abdomen.

Waist Circumference

Over 80cm indicates higher risk regardless of BMI.

Important: BMI is only a screening tool.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a healthy BMI for women?

18.5–24.9 is considered healthy.

Is BMI accurate for women?

It is a screening tool, not a diagnostic tool.

Does BMI change with age?

Yes, body composition changes with age.

What is healthy waist size?

Below 80cm is generally recommended.
