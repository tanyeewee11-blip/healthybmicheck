About HealthyBMICheck.com

Free health calculators built on peer-reviewed science

HealthyBMICheck.com is a free, independent health tools website. Our goal is simple: give everyone access to accurate, evidence-based health calculators without requiring a sign-up, subscription, or personal data.

We built this site because too many online health calculators use outdated or unverified formulas, show intrusive advertising around sensitive health results, or demand personal information to deliver a number that should be freely available. We wanted to do better.

Our Principles

🔬

Evidence-based formulas

Every calculator on this site uses a formula from peer-reviewed research or established clinical guidelines. We cite our sources.

🔒

No data collection

All calculations run in your browser. Nothing you enter is sent to our servers, stored, or shared with third parties.

⚡

Instant results

No sign-up, no email confirmation, no waiting. Enter your numbers and get your result in under a second.

📖

Explained, not just calculated

We explain what your result means, what its limitations are, and what to do next — not just a bare number.

Important Limitations

Health calculators — even well-validated ones — are population-level estimates. They are useful starting points, not medical diagnoses. Individual factors such as genetics, medications, hormonal status, body composition, and medical history can all cause your true values to differ from what a formula predicts.

⚕️ Not medical advice. The results from our calculators are for general informational purposes only. If you have health concerns, are pregnant, have a chronic illness, or are planning significant dietary or exercise changes, please consult a qualified healthcare professional.

Who Maintains This Site?

HealthyBMICheck.com is an independent project maintained by a small team passionate about making reliable health information more accessible. We are not affiliated with any hospital, pharmaceutical company, supplement brand, or health insurer. We have no financial interest in the results our calculators produce.

We review and update our calculators and articles regularly to ensure the underlying formulas and health guidance remain current with scientific consensus. If you spot an error or out-of-date information, please let us know.
