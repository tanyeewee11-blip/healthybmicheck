Why we built this

Too many online health calculators use outdated formulas, bury results behind sign-up walls, or show intrusive advertising around sensitive health information. We wanted to do better.

HealthyBMICheck.com is an independent project. We're not affiliated with any hospital, pharmaceutical company, supplement brand, or health insurer. We have no financial interest in the results our calculators produce — only in making them as accurate and useful as possible.

Every tool on this site uses a formula from peer-reviewed research or established clinical guidelines. We cite our sources, explain the limitations, and tell you what to do with your result.

Our principles

Four commitments we hold ourselves to on every page.

🔬

Evidence-based formulas

Every calculator uses a formula from peer-reviewed research or established clinical guidelines. We cite our sources on every page.

🔒

No data collection

All calculations run entirely in your browser. Nothing you enter is sent to our servers, stored, or shared with anyone.

⚡

Instant, no friction

No sign-up, no email confirmation, no paywall. Enter your numbers and get your result in under a second.

📖

Explained, not just calculated

We tell you what your result means, what its limitations are, and what to do next — not just a bare number.
